Abbotsford

Named for the bank in Abbotsford, Canada, where a twenty-three- year-old stockbroker moved a paperclip for every sales call he made — same idea as Paperclip, just for whatever tasks you add to a day instead of timed Pomodoros. One paperclip moves from the unfinished pile to the finished one each time you check something off — and a task that actually takes more than 20 minutes earns extra clips (one per 10 minutes), so the finished pile reflects real effort, not just a raw task count.
